🛒 Realm Marketplace

A comprehensive economy and community add-on for your Bedrock Realm. Everything operates via a central compass menu (or the `/marktplatz:menu` command) and relies exclusively on stable, officially released Script APIs—no beta APIs or experimental features required.

✨ Features

Auction House – List items at a price of your choice for other players to buy. Listings expire automatically after 3 weeks and are returned to you.

Order System – Instead of waiting for someone to sell an item, you can place a "buy order": specify the item, quantity, and price per unit, and the total cost is reserved immediately. Other players can fulfill your order (even partially). Orders do not expire but can be cancelled at any time.

Mailbox – Purchased items or sales proceeds that couldn't be delivered immediately (e.g., due to a full inventory or being offline) are safely stored in your mailbox.

Homes & TPA – Set up to 5 personal home points and use a player-to-player teleport request system, complete with a safety countdown that cancels if combat occurs or you move.

Bounties – Place a bounty on other players, visible on a public list. Bounties can only be claimed through an actual PvP kill.

Clans – Freely create a clan and assign a color and tag that appear on the nameplate above players' heads. Join via request; the leader manages the membership.

Random Teleport (RTP) – Safely teleport to a random location in the wilderness around the world spawn, featuring fall protection and a cooldown. Live Sidebar Dashboard – A multi-line, rotating scoreboard on the right side of the screen: the view automatically cycles every 2.5 minutes between money, bounty, job level, and clan size—displaying one line per online player, updated live and instantly whenever a change occurs. Admins can customize the title directly in-game.

Jobs – Choose from mining, farming, lumberjacking, hunting, and hitman roles. Gain levels and XP that persist even if you switch jobs; payouts increase with your level, with no upper limit.

Lottery – Buy tickets for a growing jackpot with a guaranteed minimum amount. The draw takes place every 60 minutes.

Server Events – A time-limited gathering competition (non-PvP) starts automatically every 48 hours, offering an attractive reward for the winner.

⚙️ Requirements
Minecraft Bedrock, minimum version 1.21.0
Uses a scoreboard named "money" for the currency system
